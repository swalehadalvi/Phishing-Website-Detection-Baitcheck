phishing-domains-snapshot.txt
Snapshot taken 4 Nov 2026 of the active phishing domains published by the open-source
Phishing.Database project (https://github.com/mitchellkrogza/Phishing.Database).
One domain per line, lowercase, deduplicated. Check the project's repository for its
licence and for the latest list. The India National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal does not
publish a list of fraud sites, so none of this data comes from it.
